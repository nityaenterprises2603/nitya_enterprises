About Us

Nitya Enterprises is an agriculture-focused enterprise engaged in the cultivation of processing-grade potatoes in Badaun District, Uttar Pradesh—one of India’s most suitable regions for chip- and fry-grade potato varieties. We cultivate Chipsona P -otato Series-1, Series-3, and Frysona using controlled agronomic practices, certi -fied quality seed material, and continuous field-level monitoring to meet the str -ingent requirements of the potato chips, wafers, and French fries’ industry.

Our cultivation strategy is designed with a clear focus on high dry matter content, low reducing sugars, uniform tuber size, and superior frying quality. Chipsona varieties are grown specifically for potato chips and wafers, delivering excellent slice integrity, light chip colour, and low oil absorption. Frysona, on the other hand, is cultivated for French fries and deep-frying applications, producing long, uniform tubers with a crisp exterior and fluffy internal texture after frying.

Each harvest lot—whether Chipsona or Frysona—is systematically evaluated against industry benchmarks for specific gravity, moisture content, reducing sugars, frying colour, and oil absorption. This rigorous quality assessment ensures consistent processing performance, re -sulting in light-coloured, crisp chips and fries with high recovery and controlled oil up -take, meeting the expectations of snack manufacturers, QSR suppliers, and bulk processors.
